Why your funds are on hold

  • Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance — Regulatory bodies require transaction monitoring for all cross-border fund transfers to detect and prevent money laundering activities.
  • Know Your Customer (KYC) Verification — Financial institutions must verify the identity of all parties involved in a transaction before releasing funds.
  • Fraud Prevention Protocols — Automated systems flag transactions that deviate from normal patterns to protect both senders and receivers from fraudulent activity.
  • Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) Guidelines — As a regulated entity, LemFi must comply with all FCA-mandated holding periods and verification procedures.
  • International Sanctions Screening — All transactions are screened against global sanctions lists to ensure compliance with international law.
  • Transaction Volume Assessment — First-time large transfers require additional review to establish a baseline for your account's risk profile.

How to add money to your wallet

Fund additions must be done directly through the LemFi app on your device. This is required by our banking partners for security and compliance purposes.

  1. Open the LemFi app on your phone (download it if you haven't already).
  2. Tap "Add Money" from your wallet home screen.
  3. Choose your funding source and enter an amount between $500 – $1,000.
  4. Complete the transaction. Once your wallet history meets the requirement, return here and your held funds will be released automatically.
